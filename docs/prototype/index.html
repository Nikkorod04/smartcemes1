What changed in this revision

Hierarchy & colleges

D-R1 · D-R2

Programs are broad and distributed across CAS, COE and CME. Each program contains extension projects, and each project contains activities. All four levels live under a single sidebar entry: Manage Extension Programs.

Manage Extension Programs (College) →
└ Programs →
└ Projects →
└ Activities →

Training hours & targets

D-R3 · D-R5 · D-R6

Hours are computed as trainors × trainees × days × 8, with a half day counting as 4 hours. Targets exist at University and Project level only.

Open University Targets →

Faculty Management

D-R9

Faculty Management is the engagement board — hours rendered, projects involved and lead roles per professor. The roster itself lives in the Faculty Directory, where profiles, the ranked position ladder and expertise areas are managed, and where faculty maintain their own contact details.

Open Faculty Management →
Open Faculty Directory →

Lean project view

D-R7

The old eight-KPI scorecard is gone. A project is now measured on what matters: trainors, trainees, training hours rendered, budget and activities.

Open a project hub →

AI scope guardrails

D-R8 · D-R10

The AI recommends only CESO-deliverable interventions. Out-of-scope needs are flagged as interagency interventions naming the responsible agency; the rest are suppressed.

Open AI Analysis →
Open Interagency Catalogue →

Rankings & filters

D-R9

Most active projects and most engaged faculty are ranked directly, so the Director can see where effort is concentrated at a glance. Faculty are ranked on contribution — hours rendered and project involvement — never against a personal target.
